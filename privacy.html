Privacy Policy

Personal Data Client · last updated 30 September 2026

Summary

This application is used by one person — its developer, Travis Foster — to read his own Google Account data onto his own computers. It has no other users. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share anyone's data with anyone.

What data is accessed

With the account holder's explicit OAuth consent, the app may read:

  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — files the account owner created.
  • Gmail — messages, to sort and summarise the owner's own inbox.
  • Google Calendar — events and free/busy information.
  • Google Health — sleep, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability and related fitness measurements recorded by the owner's own devices.

How it is used

Data is used only to produce summaries for the account owner's personal use — for example, turning nightly sleep and heart-rate-variability readings into a short daily record used to plan exercise. No automated decisions are made about any other person, and nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

How it is stored

Results are written to files on the owner's own computers and to a private source repository that only the owner can access. OAuth tokens are held in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain) on those machines. No data is stored on any server operated by this application, because there is no such server.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties. It is not transferred to any third-party service beyond the Google APIs the data is read from.

Google user data and limited use

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Files are kept for as long as they are useful to the owner and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at any moment from the account's Google Account permissions page, which immediately ends the app's ability to read any data.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the maintainer via github.com/TravisDFoster.
